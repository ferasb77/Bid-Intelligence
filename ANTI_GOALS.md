# Product Anti-Goals

These are architectural constraints, not deferred features. Work that moves Bid Intelligence toward one of these forms must be rejected unless the Constitution is amended under [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Proposal writer

Bid Intelligence will not make proposal generation its competitive focus. General-purpose AI and experienced consulting teams already serve that need. The platform may organize authoritative inputs and verify a completed response, but writing volume is not its measure of value.

## Generic procurement platform

Bid Intelligence will not become a broad purchasing, sourcing, vendor-management, or transaction-processing suite. It remains designed for capability-development and boutique consulting firms making evidence-intensive pursuit decisions.

## ChatGPT wrapper

Bid Intelligence will not be a thin interface around prompts. Its durable value comes from authoritative evidence, provenance, explicit contracts, structured reasoning, compliance verification, and organizational memory.

## Autonomous bidding engine

Bid Intelligence will not initiate pursuits, commit resources, accept risk, authorize submissions, or act for accountable professionals. Automation may prepare and verify; it may not assume executive authority.

## Win probability engine

Bid Intelligence will not manufacture a probability of success from incomplete observations, historical correlation, buyer signals, or opaque weights. It may show evidence and documented patterns. It must not present prediction as knowledge.

## Executive decision maker

Bid Intelligence will not decide Bid / No Bid, select strategy, approve commercial terms, choose pricing, or replace experienced consultants. Decision workspaces may organize considerations and questions, but never answer them for management.

## Black-box AI platform

Bid Intelligence will not hide evidence, reasoning, assumptions, uncertainty, alternatives, or limitations behind a score or confident narrative. Facts and inference remain distinct. Correlation is never causation. Unsupported conclusions fail closed.

## Applying an anti-goal

Reject a feature when its primary value depends on an excluded product form, even if its interface uses different language. A local convenience does not outweigh a constitutional boundary. When uncertain, apply the constitutional test in [`MANIFESTO.md`](MANIFESTO.md) and resolve conflicts through [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).
