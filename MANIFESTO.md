# The Bid Intelligence Constitution

Bid Intelligence is a **Decision Preparation Platform** for firms whose product is expertise: Capability Development, Leadership Development, Learning & Development, HR Consulting, Organizational Development, Executive Education, and Boutique Consulting Firms.

We help experienced professionals understand consequential work before they commit to it, verify their response before they submit it, and learn after the outcome is known.

**The platform prepares decisions. Humans make decisions. Human judgment is sovereign.**

## Why we exist

Professional services firms already know how to write. They have experienced consultants, proposal libraries, institutional knowledge, and access to capable general-purpose tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Writing more text is not the enduring problem.

The harder work is to establish what the opportunity requires, understand the buyer through evidence, verify that a proposal answers the request, and retain what the organization learns across pursuits. Bid Intelligence exists to make that work rigorous, traceable, and reusable.

Proposal generation is outside our competitive focus. We complement general-purpose writing tools; we do not compete with them.

## The four pillars

Every product capability must strengthen at least one pillar.

1. **Opportunity Intelligence — understand the opportunity.** Establish requirements, evaluation structure, submission mechanics, dates, commercial facts, deliverables, dependencies, ambiguity, and conflict from authoritative source material.
2. **Buyer Intelligence — understand the buyer.** Organize observable buyer evidence and distinguish documented patterns from interpretation. Correlation must never be presented as causation.
3. **Proposal Compliance Intelligence — verify the proposal.** Trace the completed response to requirements, evaluation criteria, evidence, commercial obligations, and submission rules. Completeness is not a prediction of success.
4. **Organizational Intelligence — learn from historical bids.** Turn RFPs, proposals, outcomes, debriefs, and lessons into durable organizational memory. Learning across many decisions matters more than an isolated prediction.

A feature that cannot identify its pillar does not belong in the platform.

## Human judgment

The system may reduce the effort required to understand, compare, navigate, coordinate, and verify. It may expose what deserves attention. It may ask questions that require executive judgment. It must not make the judgment.

Bid / No Bid, commercial acceptance, organizational commitment, strategy, pricing, and submission authorization remain accountable human decisions. The system must never disguise an automated conclusion as an unavoidable answer.

## AI and evidence

AI may perform extraction, organization, comparison, evidence linking, pattern recognition, and transparent reasoning. These capabilities are valuable only when their authority is explicit.

Source facts remain distinct from deterministic computations. Computations remain distinct from inference. Assumptions, limitations, alternatives, conflicts, and unknowns remain visible. Meaningful reasoning must identify the evidence it uses and the uncertainty it cannot remove.

AI must never make executive decisions, decide Bid / No Bid, predict winners, replace experienced consultants, or produce unexplained recommendations. If a professional cannot inspect why an observation or inference exists, it does not belong in the product.

## The Moneyball principle

Bid Intelligence applies the durable lesson of Moneyball to professional pursuits:

- evidence over intuition;
- organizational learning over isolated prediction;
- observations over hidden scoring.

Measurement supports expertise; it does not replace it. Correlation is not causation. An observation is not a recommendation. A score is not a decision.

## The constitutional test

Before implementation, every contribution must answer:

1. Which pillar does this strengthen?
2. Which burden of understanding, comparison, coordination, verification, or learning does it reduce?
3. Does it preserve sovereign human judgment?
4. Does it keep evidence, computation, inference, and decision authority distinct?
5. Would an experienced professional use it during a consequential pursuit?

If the answers do not support this Constitution, the contribution must change or stop. The binding exclusions are defined in [`ANTI_GOALS.md`](ANTI_GOALS.md), amendment rules in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md), and AI duties in [`AGENT.md`](AGENT.md).

## Our measure of success

We do not measure the product by generated words, executed prompts, or automated decisions. We measure whether experienced professionals can say:

- “This helped us understand the opportunity.”
- “This helped us understand the buyer.”
- “This helped us verify our proposal.”
- “This helped us learn from previous bids.”

We are building intelligence that elevates professional judgment. Everything in this repository exists to serve that purpose.
