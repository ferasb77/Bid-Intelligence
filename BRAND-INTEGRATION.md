# Bid Intelligence brand integration

Bid Intelligence is presented as an endorsed Enable My Growth application developed by Feras Banna.

## Positioning

**Product name:** Bid Intelligence  
**Edition:** Decision & Proposal Platform  
**Descriptor:** A structured environment for examining bid decisions, evidence, and proposal readiness before committing resources.  
**Parent-brand line:** Perspective changes what becomes possible.

The product supports commercial and proposal judgment. It does not make the bid/no-bid decision, guarantee compliance, or replace accountable review by the bid team.
