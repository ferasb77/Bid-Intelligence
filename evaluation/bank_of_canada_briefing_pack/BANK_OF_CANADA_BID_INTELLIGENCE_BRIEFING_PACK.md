# Volume 1 — Executive Opportunity Brief

## Opportunity at a Glance
- **Opportunity:** RFP 2026-026 - Talent, Learning and Organizational Development Services
- **Buyer:** Bank of Canada
- **Reference:** RFP 2026-026
- **Purpose:** Bank of Canada seeks qualified service providers to deliver on-demand talent, learning, and organizational development services across three primary service categories: (1) Learning and Development Programs—customized and off-the-shelf learning solutions ranging from instructor-led to blended delivery for leader development; (2) HR Advisory Services—strategic human resources and organizational development consulting including workforce assessments, succession planning, and implementation recommendations; and (3) Facilitation and Team Effectiveness—customized facilitation and team effectiveness engagements tailored to organizational needs. All services must be delivered bilingually (English and French), with digital content meeting accessibility standards (WCAG, EN 301 549). Service providers must maintain security clearances and demonstrate relevant experience with organizations of 1,000+ employees.
- **Contract term:** 3 years with optional 2-year extension, subject to Bank satisfaction and mutual agreement
- **Procurement model:** Single Contract

## What the Buyer Wants
- **Overall intent:** Bank of Canada seeks qualified service providers to deliver on-demand talent, learning, and organizational development services across three primary service categories: (1) Learning and Development Programs—customized and off-the-shelf learning solutions ranging from instructor-led to blended delivery for leader development; (2) HR Advisory Services—strategic human resources and organizational development consulting including workforce assessments, succession planning, and implementation recommendations; and (3) Facilitation and Team Effectiveness—customized facilitation and team effectiveness engagements tailored to organizational needs. All services must be delivered bilingually (English and French), with digital content meeting accessibility standards (WCAG, EN 301 549). Service providers must maintain security clearances and demonstrate relevant experience with organizations of 1,000+ employees.
- **Consulting domain:** Learning and Development Programs
- **Consulting domain:** HR Advisory Services
- **Consulting domain:** Facilitation and Team Effectiveness
- **Relationship between service areas:** The procurement groups the requested capability into 3 service areas: Learning and Development Programs; HR Advisory Services; Facilitation and Team Effectiveness.
- **Expected outcome — Learning and Development Programs:** Customized and off-the-shelf learning solutions including needs analysis, curriculum design, pilot delivery, program delivery across 21 cohorts (420 leaders over 3 years), and evaluation.
- **Expected outcome — HR Advisory Services:** Strategic consulting including discovery/assessment (20 Bank documents, workforce datasets, 15 stakeholder interviews, validation session), analysis and recommendations (workforce risk analysis, succession/talent sustainability planning, implementation roadmap), and reporting/executive presentation (1 final English report, 1 ninety-minute executive presentation).
- **Expected outcome — Facilitation and Team Effectiveness Engagements:** Customized facilitation and team effectiveness services including analysis phase (1 kickoff, 7 interviews, documentation review), design phase (approach, 1 planning meeting, 2 workshop agendas, materials), delivery phase (2 four-hour in-person workshops with 7 participants), and follow-up/evaluation phase (post-engagement report, feedback survey, recommendations).
