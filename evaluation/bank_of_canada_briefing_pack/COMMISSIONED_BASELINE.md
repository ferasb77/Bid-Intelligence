## 7. Known architectural debt (frozen with the baseline, not silently forgotten)

1. **Stage D** — high input-token volume, ~146k input tokens per diagnostic call observed. Not optimized in this freeze.
2. **Opportunity Intelligence** — does not independently understand VERIFIED/PARTIAL/UNVERIFIED for most fact families. Currently conservative enough not to violate commissioning, but architectural debt.
3. **Opportunity Intelligence integration** — currently standalone / no live application consumer.
4. **Buyer Brief v1** — external organizational-fact brief only; procurement-context/interpretation sections intentionally empty.
5. **Executive Opportunity Understanding** — `DetailRegister.conflicts` is dead/unrouted; conflicts currently represented through `detail_register.unknowns`.
6. **Legacy commissioning script** — old Executive Briefing Pack script targets the superseded 15-document corpus/live LLM workflow (Section 4).
7. **DOCX evidence granularity** — hierarchical SECTION binding now works, but the Evidence Adapter remains coarser than semantic section labels for many DOCX documents. Documented as an architectural limitation even though binding is constitutionally safe now.
8. **Advisory conflicts** — `CONF-EVAL-N` remains Stage-C-local advisory identity, not a governed citable entity. This distinction must remain explicit.

## 8. Known non-blocking limitations

- `canonical_opportunity.py`'s `_source_segment` stops at the first `_locator_matches`-satisfying marker rather than searching exhaustively — a documented, safe-direction (under- not over-verification) divergence.
- `apply_legacy_conflict_fallback`'s text-keyword field matching could, if it ever fires for evaluation_criteria-shaped legacy conflicts, inject an ungoverned ordinal id into a resolved field's `conflict_ids` — confirmed dormant/inactive in this corpus.
- `float`/`DECIMAL` support is still missing from 6 other publication modules' own independent `_semantic_value` copies (fixed only in `opportunity_structure_publication.py`, where it was actually hit).
