# Operational Customer Personas

These personas describe work and accountability. One person may perform several roles in a boutique firm.

## CEO / Managing Partner

- **Responsibilities:** Own pursuit strategy, resource commitment, commercial exposure, reputation, and final accountability.
- **Objectives:** Commit selectively, protect the firm, and build a stronger long-term market position.
- **Primary questions:** Is this opportunity aligned with the firm? What is known, uncertain, and consequential? What requires executive judgment?
- **Information needed:** Opportunity and buyer evidence, conflicts, capability and capacity input, commercial review, proposal compliance, and prior outcomes.
- **Platform touchpoints:** Decision workspace, executive review, outcome, organizational learning.
- **Decisions made:** Bid / No Bid, strategic posture, material exceptions, commercial acceptance, final authorization.
- **Not delegated:** Accountability for commitment, risk acceptance, strategy, or submission release.
- **Pain points:** Fragmented briefs, optimistic narratives, hidden uncertainty, late escalation, and lost institutional memory.
- **Success measures:** Faster informed deliberation, explicit rationale, fewer surprises, and evidence that later pursuits use earlier learning.

## Bid Manager

- **Responsibilities:** Control the pursuit process, source package, requirements, schedule, owners, compliance, and submission.
- **Objectives:** Maintain one reliable view of the opportunity and bring decisions to the right people at the right time.
- **Primary questions:** What must happen, by when, with what evidence, and where are the gaps or conflicts?
- **Information needed:** Authoritative documents, requirements, evaluation structure, submission rules, dates, clarifications, assignments, and readiness evidence.
- **Platform touchpoints:** Intake, Opportunity Intelligence, Buyer Intelligence, compliance, executive review, submission, and debrief.
- **Decisions made:** Workflow coordination, escalation, review timing, and operational controls within delegated authority.
- **Not delegated:** Interpretation of ambiguous source material, acceptance of material risk, or release authority outside their mandate.
- **Pain points:** Version confusion, manual traceability, repeated checking, scattered ownership, and late discoveries.
- **Success measures:** Complete source control, early visibility of issues, accountable hand-offs, and zero avoidable submission defects.

## Proposal Manager / Proposal Writer

- **Responsibilities:** Shape the response, coordinate contributors, maintain coherence, and ensure the draft addresses the request.
- **Objectives:** Produce a clear, credible proposal grounded in approved evidence and current requirements.
- **Primary questions:** What must each section accomplish? Which evidence supports each claim? What remains unanswered?
- **Information needed:** Requirements, criteria, selected prior material, case evidence, firm methods, buyer context, and review findings.
- **Platform touchpoints:** Opportunity brief, content selection, drafting hand-off, compliance review, and submission package.
- **Decisions made:** Structure, narrative, writing assignments, and editorial choices within the approved strategy.
- **Not delegated:** Truthfulness of claims, ethical reuse, confidentiality judgments, or acceptance of unresolved compliance gaps.
- **Pain points:** Untraceable requirements, stale content, conflicting inputs, weak evidence, and reviews based on memory.
- **Success measures:** A coherent draft with traceable coverage, fewer review cycles, and no unsupported claims.

## Senior Consultant

- **Responsibilities:** Define the delivery approach, test feasibility, contribute subject expertise, and review substantive quality.
- **Objectives:** Ensure the proposed work responds to the client’s need and can be delivered credibly.
- **Primary questions:** What problem is the buyer asking us to solve? Are scope, method, deliverables, and assumptions coherent?
- **Information needed:** Requirements, buyer context, deliverables, dependencies, evaluation criteria, commercial constraints, and relevant experience.
- **Platform touchpoints:** Opportunity and Buyer Intelligence, prior-proposal selection, drafting, compliance, and executive review.
- **Decisions made:** Recommended approach, work design, evidence selection, and substantive revisions within their mandate.
- **Not delegated:** Professional judgment about method, feasibility, ethics, or claims made under their expertise.
- **Pain points:** Late involvement, compressed source material, hidden constraints, and reuse without context.
- **Success measures:** Feasible commitments, strong alignment between need and method, and fewer substantive corrections late in review.

## Subject Matter Expert

- **Responsibilities:** Validate specialist claims, credentials, methods, standards, and technical feasibility.
- **Objectives:** Contribute precise expertise without having to reconstruct the entire procurement package.
- **Primary questions:** What exactly needs specialist review? Which source requirement or claim does it support?
- **Information needed:** Focused source evidence, relevant requirement, draft claim, assumptions, and requested decision.
- **Platform touchpoints:** Assigned evidence and requirements, proposal review, clarification, and compliance exceptions.
- **Decisions made:** Whether specialist content is accurate, supportable, and professionally acceptable.
- **Not delegated:** Certification of expertise, technical sign-off, or approval of unsupported claims.
- **Pain points:** Vague requests, missing context, duplicated review, and unclear ownership.
- **Success measures:** Focused review effort, traceable specialist approval, and accurate claims.

## Commercial / Finance

- **Responsibilities:** Assess economics, pricing constraints, contractual exposure, payment structure, and financial approvals.
- **Objectives:** Make commercial implications explicit before the firm commits or submits.
- **Primary questions:** What commercial facts are authoritative? What is uncertain? Which terms require acceptance, clarification, or negotiation?
- **Information needed:** Canonical money and term observations, verified clauses, scope, dates, resource assumptions, pricing inputs, and unresolved conflicts.
- **Platform touchpoints:** Opportunity Intelligence, commercial review, executive review, and outcome record.
- **Decisions made:** Financial analysis, pricing approval within authority, exceptions, and escalation recommendations.
- **Not delegated:** Acceptance of material exposure, final price commitment, or invented interpretation of unclear clauses.
- **Pain points:** Commercial facts buried in documents, dictation by deadlines, inconsistent assumptions, and unsupported risk labels.
- **Success measures:** Early review, traceable facts, explicit assumptions, authorized commitments, and fewer post-award surprises.
