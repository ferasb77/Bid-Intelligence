# Product Principles

1. **Understanding before writing.** Establish the opportunity and its uncertainty before producing a response.
2. **Evidence before inference.** Every meaningful interpretation must remain traceable to evidence; unsupported certainty has no place in the product.
3. **Facts remain facts.** Source facts, computations, inference, hypotheses, recommendations, and human decisions retain distinct authority.
4. **Human judgment is sovereign.** The platform prepares decisions and questions. Accountable professionals decide.
5. **Unknowns remain visible.** Missing evidence, ambiguity, conflict, and unavailable information are product outputs, not defects to conceal with narrative.
6. **Transparency over prediction.** Show observations, relationships, assumptions, alternatives, and limitations instead of opaque forecasts or scores.
7. **Reduce cognitive effort without replacing thought.** Reduce the work of finding, comparing, tracing, coordinating, and verifying. Preserve the work of judgment.
8. **Professional expertise is amplified.** Product behavior should make experienced consultants more effective, not imitate or displace their accountability.
9. **Organizational learning compounds.** Design each pursuit so its evidence, decisions, outcomes, and lessons can improve later work.
10. **Correlation is not causation.** Historical patterns may inform inquiry; they do not prove buyer intent or future outcomes.
11. **Completeness is not success.** Compliance can be verified. Winning cannot be inferred from compliance alone.
12. **Authority survives transformation.** Summaries, projections, analyses, and views must not mutate authoritative facts or discard provenance.
13. **The customer is intentionally narrow.** Product choices serve Capability Development and Boutique Consulting Firms, including leadership, learning, HR, organizational development, and executive education practices.
14. **General-purpose tools remain part of the workflow.** Bid Intelligence complements established writing tools and does not reproduce their role.
15. **Failure is explicit.** When evidence or validation is insufficient, the product stops or reports the limitation rather than silently inventing, truncating, or repairing meaning.

Use these principles together. No single principle authorizes a feature that conflicts with the Constitution or an anti-goal. When principles create a genuine trade-off, preserve human judgment and evidence authority first, then resolve the product choice through [`GOVERNANCE.md`](../../GOVERNANCE.md).
