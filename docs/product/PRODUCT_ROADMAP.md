# Product Roadmap

Horizons express dependency and maturity, not promised dates. Work advances when the preceding evidence, contracts, and operating behavior are trustworthy.

## Foundation

Establish the authority and reliability required for every pillar:

- deterministic ingestion and source provenance;
- explicit separation of facts, computations, inference, hypotheses, and human decisions;
- canonical opportunity facts and conflict preservation;
- stable contracts, validation, replay, and evidence-linked outputs;
- constitutional product governance and anti-goals.

The Foundation is maintained continuously. Later capability cannot justify weakening it.

## Current

Make Opportunity Intelligence operational as the first complete pillar:

- extract and reconcile authoritative procurement facts;
- organize requirements, evaluation, submissions, dates, commercial clauses, and deliverables;
- expose provenance, ambiguity, conflicts, and evidence gaps;
- compute individual structural indicators without composite scores;
- present validated analysis for human deliberation through the Decision Workspace.

Current work should improve trust, completeness, usability, and real-world acceptance of this flow before expanding the product surface.

## Near Term

Extend the evidence model around the existing pursuit workflow:

- develop Buyer Intelligence from governed, observable buyer evidence;
- strengthen Proposal Compliance Intelligence against authoritative opportunity requirements;
- connect selected prior proposals to the current opportunity while preserving human selection and confidentiality;
- improve cross-role review, management questions, and traceable hand-offs;
- capture decisions and outcomes as accountable human records.

These capabilities must preserve analyst boundaries and must not infer buyer preference, probability of success, or autonomous pursuit direction.

## Medium Term

Create the first useful Organizational Intelligence loops:

- link historical RFPs to their proposals and outcomes;
- incorporate buyer debriefs as authoritative evidence with clear source identity;
- compare recurring requirements, compliance gaps, commercial structures, and delivery themes;
- distinguish portfolio observations from hypotheses and causal claims;
- help teams retrieve relevant experience and lessons for a new pursuit.

The product should show what recurs and what remains uncertain. Humans determine why it occurred and what practice should change.

## Long Term

Build a durable organizational learning system across:

- historical RFPs;
- historical proposals;
- win/loss outcomes;
- buyer debriefs;
- documented decisions, assumptions, and lessons.

The long-term experience should allow a firm to enter a pursuit with more of its relevant memory intact, inspect how present evidence relates to prior experience, and conduct better executive deliberation. Learning should compound without becoming a win-probability model, hidden score, or substitute for professional judgment.

## Horizon discipline

Roadmap work must identify the pillar strengthened, customer-workflow stage served, authoritative inputs, human owner, and learning value. Capabilities that primarily generate proposals, automate Bid / No Bid, predict winners, or generalize into procurement administration remain outside every horizon.

This roadmap changes through product-governance review. It does not authorize implementation that conflicts with [`PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md`](PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md) or the constitutional layer.
