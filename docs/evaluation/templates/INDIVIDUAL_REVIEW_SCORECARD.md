## Qualitative feedback

1. What surprised you?

2. What would you remove?

3. What information did you still have to search for?

4. What section created the greatest value?

5. What would prevent you from distributing this document internally?

6. What decision did this document help you make?

7. Which statement did you trust least, and why?

8. Which unknown or ambiguity was missing?

9. Which management question improved the discussion?

10. What did your role need that the brief did not provide?
