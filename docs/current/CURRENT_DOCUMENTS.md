## Note: a handful of self-declared "architecture" documents are NOT here

Several `_ARCHITECTURE.md`-named root files were moved to
`docs/archive/proposed/` because they explicitly self-declare `Status:
Proposed` in their own metadata (e.g. `BID_WORKSPACE_ARCHITECTURE.md`,
`BID_LIFECYCLE_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `BUYER_EVIDENCE_ACQUISITION_ARCHITECTURE.md`,
`BUYER_RETRIEVAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`) — the filename alone is not a reliable
signal of current authority in this repository; always check
`DOCUMENT_AUTHORITY_MAP.json` or the document's own metadata table.
