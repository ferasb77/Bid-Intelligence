# York P27-070 Decision-Brief Benchmark

## A. Product Purpose

### 1. Authoritative Product Mission
The primary purpose of Bid Intelligence UNDERSTAND is:
> **"Provide an evidence-based decision brief to the bid decision maker."**

The primary report is **NOT**:
- A procurement summary or metadata sheet
- A compliance checklist
- A legal contract digest
- An internal system or pipeline audit
- A dump of canonical database objects
- A dump of raw specialist findings or execution traces

### 2. Core Decision-Maker Questions
Every page and section of the primary brief must directly help an executive or bid lead answer:
1. **What is the buyer actually buying?** (Substantive operational scope and business intent beyond commodity labels).
2. **Why is this opportunity relevant / strategically meaningful?** (Buyer mandate, institutional priorities, funding or program drivers).
3. **What kind of provider is the buyer looking for?** (Organizational profile, delivery posture, cultural fit, team depth).
4. **What technical capabilities and credentials matter?** (Non-negotiable skills, subject matter authority, methodologies).
5. **How will the proposal be judged?** (True mathematical scoring structure, hidden thresholds, evaluator priorities).
6. **What must we prove with evidence?** (Proof burden, case studies, named CVs, verifiable metrics).
7. **What could materially weaken or block a bid?** (Disqualifying gates, restrictive commercial clauses, capacity choke-points).
8. **What should the bid team resolve before writing?** (Go/no-go discriminators, scoping tradeoffs, clarification questions).

### 3. Length and Executive Design Contract
- **Target Length**: 6–10 pages total (preferred 7–8 pages).
- **Format**: Single stand-alone primary **Bid Intelligence Brief**.
- **Optional Supporting Asset**: Detailed Evidence Appendix / Source Detail (separate document containing comprehensive requirement registers, full legal clause catalogs, and specialist extraction traces).
- **Executive Rule**: Maximum decision density per page. A busy bid executive must be able to absorb the complete strategic context, risks, and proof burdens within 5–10 minutes.

## F. Evaluation Intelligence

### 1. Structural Scoring Model
The written submission is evaluated on a **100-point total scale**, strictly bifurcated between Technical Qualifications (80 points) and Financial Pricing (20 points):

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TOTAL EVALUATION (100 Points)                                                     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  |
  +---> TECHNICAL QUALIFICATIONS (80 Points / 80% Weight)
  |       |
  |       +---> Stage 1: Methodology & Workplan (Section 4.3 / 4.4)
  |       |     Points: 45 Points (56.3% of Technical Score)
  |       |     Focus: Step-by-step delivery, curriculum framework, timeline, AI/Sales
  |       |
  |       +---> Stage 2: Qualifications & Experience (Section 4.1 / 4.2)
  |             Points: 35 Points (43.7% of Technical Score)
  |             Focus: SME track record, named team CVs, deep bench resilience, refs
  |
  +---> FINANCIAL PRICING (20 Points / 20% Weight)
          |
          +---> Stage 3: Pricing Schedule (Section 10.0)
                Points: 20 Points
                Focus: All-inclusive fixed cost + blended hourly rate; lowest price formula
```

### 2. Shortlist Presentations & Demonstrations (Conditional Stage)
- **Status**: Conditional ("If required by York University").
- **Weight**: 20 Additional Points (Evaluated separately if invoked).
- **Anticipated Timing**: Week of November 16, 2026.
- **Implication**: Only top-scoring written proposals will be invited to demonstrate sample instructional delivery and mentor chemistry.

### 3. Critical Scoring Rules and Thresholds
- **Mandatory Minimum Technical Threshold**: Bidders must achieve a passing technical score (typically 75% or 60/80 points) to have their financial pricing envelope opened.
- **Price Evaluation Formula**: Mathematical inverse formula ($Points = 20 \times \frac{LowestPrice}{BidderPrice}$).
- **Tie-Breaking Rule**: In the event of a tie score after technical and financial evaluation, the award is decided first by the **highest points awarded in the Pricing category**; if still tied, a formal coin toss is conducted.
- **Zero Criteria Duplication**: Evaluation criteria must never be duplicated across sub-tables or presented as separate ad-hoc scoring gates.

While commercial and administrative terms must remain subordinate to technical capability in the executive brief, the following items represent material deal-breakers or operational constraints:

### 1. Hard Submission Gates (Disqualification Risks)
- **Mandatory MERX Registration**: The bidder MUST be formally registered with MERX (`www.merx.com`) and have acquired the official RFP package under their registered entity name prior to RFP closing. Failure to do so results in **immediate rejection without evaluation**.
- **Bonfire File Architecture**: Exactly six (6) distinct PDF files must be uploaded to designated slots on `yorku.bonfirehub.ca`:

  2. *Methodology & Workplan* (Sections 4.3 / 4.4)

  4. *References* (Section 4.7)

### 2. Commercial & Operational Watch-outs

- **Payment Method Acceptance**: Supplier must accept corporate Purchasing Card (PCard) or Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) on standard Net 30/60 terms.
- **Subcontracting Restrictions**: Zero assignment or subcontracting of instructional delivery is permitted without express prior written consent from York University Procurement.
- **Accessibility (AODA) Compliance**: Bidder must warrant compliance with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and provide staff training verification records upon request.

## K. Evidence & Epistemic Rules

To preserve absolute procurement integrity, all Bid Intelligence reports must strictly adhere to four distinct epistemic tiers:

```
[ INFORMATION CLASS ]               [ DEFINITION & SOURCE OF TRUTH ]
1. BUYER-ISSUED FACT           -->   Verbatim or directly normalized fact extracted strictly from
                                    official RFP documents, schedules, or buyer addenda.
2. PUBLIC BUYER INTELLIGENCE   -->   Independently verified institutional data from official buyer
                                    websites (.edu, .ca, annual reports, public mandates).
3. BID INTELLIGENCE            -->   Algorithmic synthesis, scoring risk deductions, evaluation
   INTERPRETATION                   weight inferences, or capture team advisory recommendations.
4. UNKNOWN / NEEDS             -->   Critical item explicitly absent from buyer documents or
   CONFIRMATION                     requiring pre-bid clarification.
```

### Absolute Prohibitions:
- **No Blurring of Tiers**: An AI inference must never be disguised as a buyer-stated fact.
- **No Hallucinated Requirements**: Never invent a client requirement or silently repair an omission.
- **No Conversion of Extraction Failures into Buyer Omissions**: If an extraction prompt misses a section that is present in the source PDF, the report must never state *"The buyer did not specify this requirement."*
- **No Leaked Internal IDs**: Internal database keys, specialist prefixes (`REQ-13`, `SPECIALIST_FULL:4`), or pipeline execution tokens must never appear in customer-facing prose.

## L. Report Quality Gates

A generated Bid Intelligence Brief will be graded as **FAIL** if any of the following quality defects occur:
1. **Omission of Core Ecosystem Signal**: Fails to highlight Canadian SME and start-up ecosystem experience as a primary qualification.
2. **Omission of Team Resilience**: Fails to surface the "deep bench / no single point of failure" and named CV requirements.
3. **Omission of Core Subject Capabilities**: Fails to capture B2B sales playbooks, applied AI integration, and leadership coaching.
4. **Omission of Curriculum Preference**: Fails to identify York's strong preference for an existing, proven curriculum framework.
5. **Methodology Scoring Subordination**: Fails to identify Methodology & Workplan as the single largest scoring category (45 points / 56.3% of technical score).
6. **Displacement by Commodity Terms**: Allocates more visual space or priority to standard insurance, AODA, or portal mechanics than to substantive technical delivery requirements.
7. **Phantom Buyer Intelligence**: Displays empty buyer overview templates or repeats the buyer title ("York University") without providing external organizational mandate or context.
8. **Duplicate Evaluation Criteria**: Generates repeated criteria rows or fragments criteria into conflicting tables.
9. **Leaked Pipeline Artifacts**: Exposes internal debug keys, specialist IDs, or database entity names in narrative text.
