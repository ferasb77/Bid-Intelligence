## 1. What is actually being published, and why it is not a "Requirements Register"

Commissioning found a gap named by its symptom — three unresolved `SupportedEntityType` values. Naming the *owner* after that symptom would repeat the mistake `DOMAIN_MODEL.md` warns against implicitly throughout: names should describe what a layer *is responsible for*, not what happened to be missing when the gap was found.

Reconstructing responsibility from repository evidence rather than the symptom:

- `MANIFESTO.md`, Pillar 1, states the actual scope directly: *"Opportunity Intelligence — understand the opportunity. Establish requirements, evaluation structure, submission mechanics, dates, commercial facts, deliverables, dependencies, ambiguity, and conflict from authoritative source material."*
- Of that list, "dates" already belongs to Canonical Opportunity (its `MILESTONE` family). "Ambiguity" and "conflict" are already produced deterministically by Stage C. What remains — **requirements, evaluation structure, submission mechanics, commercial facts, deliverables** — is exactly the content Stage B already normalizes and Stage C already reconciles, under the keys `requirements`, `evaluation_criteria`, `submission_rules`, `commercial_clauses`, and `deliverables`, but which never passes through `canonical_opportunity.py`'s reconciliation (that function processes only `typed_observations`, whose families are `IDENTITY`, `MILESTONE`, `CONTRACT_TERM`, `MONETARY`, `PROCUREMENT_MECHANIC`, `DOCUMENT_ROLE` — a disjoint set).
- `docs/architecture/DOMAIN_MODEL.md` already assigns architectural ownership for four of these five kinds to *"Opportunity Intelligence domain authority, grounded in Canonical Truth"* — Requirement, Evaluation Criterion, Deliverable are stated this way explicitly; Commercial Clause is assigned to *"Canonical Truth owns the fact; qualified humans own legal and commercial judgment."* Notably, none of the five is assigned to Canonical Opportunity, and `CANONICAL_OPPORTUNITY_PUBLICATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §5 excludes them from it by name: *"normalized requirements; evaluation entities or evaluation hierarchy objects; submission entities, artifacts, rules, pathways, or projections; Contract Hygiene entities, including admitted deliverables and commercial clauses."*
- Yet `opportunity_intelligence.py` — the module `DOMAIN_MODEL.md`'s ownership language points to — does not publish these entities either. It only *consumes* them, building `EvidenceSupport` references that presume a governed target already exists (`_entity_type()` maps each section to `SupportedEntityType.REQUIREMENT` / `.DELIVERABLE` / `.COMMERCIAL_CLAUSE`; `_record_id()` already derives stable IDs for them, reusing `req_id`, `deliverable_id`, `clause_id`, `criterion_id` when Stage A supplied one).

The pattern this reveals is exact: **Opportunity Intelligence, as a domain, is missing the same foundational/interpretive split that Evidence and Canonical Opportunity already have.** Evidence (raw) feeds Canonical Opportunity (reconciled identity/metadata facts) feeds nothing interpretive above it directly — interpretation happens elsewhere. Symmetrically, Evidence should feed a reconciled, canonical layer for procurement *structure*, which in turn should feed `opportunity_intelligence.py`'s interpretive analysis. That reconciled, canonical, structural layer is the missing owner. It already exists as *data* (Stage B/C's output); it has never existed as a *governed, published domain*.

**This is larger than a "Requirements Register."** A register is a passive catalog. What is actually missing is a full peer to Canonical Opportunity: an owner with the same rigor — deterministic reconciliation, stable identity, conflict tracking, supersession, provenance, immutable publication — applied to five cross-referential entity families instead of six. Naming it after only one of those families (requirements) would misstate its scope and invite exactly the kind of narrow, incomplete implementation this discovery exercise exists to prevent.

**Proposed name: Opportunity Structure.** It sits in the same naming family as Canonical Opportunity, Opportunity Intelligence, Opportunity Orchestration, and Executive Opportunity Understanding, and its name states its actual job: publishing the normalized *structure* a solicitation establishes — what is required, how it is judged, what must be delivered, what commercial terms apply, and how a response must be submitted — as distinct from Canonical Opportunity's *identity* content (who, when, how much, what kind of procurement) and Opportunity Intelligence's *interpretation* of what that structure means.

## 2. One owner or several?

**One owner, five object classes**, for reasons grounded directly in existing precedent and in the data's own shape:

1. **Precedent.** Canonical Opportunity already publishes six distinct families (`IDENTITY`, `MILESTONE`, `CONTRACT_TERM`, `MONETARY`, `PROCUREMENT_MECHANIC`, `DOCUMENT_ROLE`) as one domain under one ledger, one input digest, one publication contract. Splitting Requirement/Evaluation-Criterion/Commercial-Clause/Deliverable/Submission-Rule into five separate owners would apply a different, inconsistent rule to structurally identical content emerging from the identical Stage B/C computation.
2. **Shared authoritative input.** All five families are produced by the same `normalize_package_facts` / `reconcile_package_facts` pass, over the same package, with the same authoritative-input digest. Splitting them would mean re-deriving or duplicating that binding five times for what is a single deterministic computation.
3. **Cross-reference density.** `DOMAIN_MODEL.md` states Requirement's relationships directly: *"may relate to Evaluation Criteria, Deliverables, Proposal Responses, Compliance, and Coverage."* A Requirement is commonly assessed by an Evaluation Criterion and satisfied by a Deliverable, within the same document. Five separate owners would force every one of those relationships through cross-owner governed-reference resolution for what is, in truth, one coherent structural model.
4. **`QUALIFICATION_REQUIREMENT` is not a sixth class.** Per `extractor.py`'s own taxonomy, a "qualification gate" is a `Requirement` whose `category == "Mandatory"` and `requirement_type == "Supplier Qualification"` — a typed attribute combination on the *same* Requirement object, not a distinct entity. Treating it as a separate owner would contradict the extraction schema it is meant to publish faithfully, and would repeat the exact `category`-vs-`requirement_type` conflation the Stage A prompt itself goes out of its way to forbid (*"Do NOT classify a requirement as 'Supplier Qualification' merely because its category is 'Mandatory'"*).

## 3. Exact constitutional responsibility

**Owns:** the deterministically normalized, deduplicated, and conflict-checked content of the `requirements`, `evaluation_criteria`, `commercial_clauses`, `deliverables`, and `submission_rules` sections of Stage B/C output — their stable identities (reusing the IDs Stage A already assigns: `req_id`, `criterion_id`, `clause_id`, `deliverable_id`, `submission_rule_id`, falling back to the deterministic hash-based scheme `opportunity_intelligence.py`'s `_record_id` already uses when Stage A did not supply one); their classification fields (category, requirement_type, artifact_type, clause_kind, evaluation_role, etc. — all already controlled vocabularies, none invented here); their provenance (source_refs, evidence linkage); their cross-references to each other; and the subset of Stage C's detected conflicts that concern them.

**Never owns:** raw Evidence content or excerpts (Evidence's exclusive domain); `IDENTITY`/`MILESTONE`/`CONTRACT_TERM`/`MONETARY`/`PROCUREMENT_MECHANIC`/`DOCUMENT_ROLE` facts (Canonical Opportunity's exclusive domain, unchanged by this proposal); any interpretation, confidence, computed fact, hypothesis, inference, Unknown, Management Question, or Clarification Question about what the structure means (Opportunity Intelligence's exclusive domain); Proposal content or Compliance/Coverage assessment (Proposal Compliance Intelligence's domain, per `MANIFESTO.md` Pillar 3, not yet built); any Human Decision.

**Publishes:** immutable, individually identified `REQUIREMENT`, `EVALUATION_CRITERION`, `COMMERCIAL_CLAUSE`, `DELIVERABLE`, and `SUBMISSION_RULE` objects, exposing every semantic field a consumer needs to interpret each one without inference, their governed evidence relationships, their cross-references to each other and to Canonical Opportunity (e.g., a submission-deadline dependency), and their conflict-membership state.

**Consumers:** Opportunity Intelligence (closing the exact gap commissioning found — its `EvidenceSupport` references for `REQUIREMENT`/`COMMERCIAL_CLAUSE`/`DELIVERABLE` finally resolve against something real); Opportunity Orchestration (admitting it as a fourth owner alongside Evidence, Canonical Opportunity, and Opportunity Intelligence Publications into one bounded operation); Executive Opportunity Understanding (organizing its content for human review, exactly as it already organizes Canonical Opportunity and Opportunity Intelligence content); Proposal Compliance Intelligence, in the future, checking a Proposal against these exact governed Requirement, Evaluation Criterion, and Deliverable objects rather than re-deriving them.

**Authority boundary created:** this owner becomes the sole authority for *what the procurement structurally demands, how it will be judged, and what a response must deliver and submit*. No downstream consumer may re-derive, reinterpret, or restate a Requirement's text, category, or classification — they may only reference it. It does not decide compliance (Pillar 3's job, not yet built) and does not interpret significance, risk, or implication (Opportunity Intelligence's job, already built and already respecting this boundary by only consuming, never asserting, these facts).

## 4. The correct abstraction

Evaluated against the candidates posed: not "normalized procurement facts" (too generic — indistinguishable from what Canonical Opportunity already calls itself); not "procurement obligations" (too narrow — an Evaluation Criterion is a measurement method, not an obligation); not "procurement semantics" (too abstract for what is, concretely, five typed record classes); "procurement requirements" alone undercounts four of the five families. The most precise fit is **procurement structure**: the normalized, reconciled shape of what a solicitation establishes for a compliant response — what is required, how it is scored, what must be delivered, on what commercial terms, and by what submission mechanics. This is "something broader" than any single item on the list, in the specific sense that it is the union of five structurally-related families under one reconciliation authority, mirroring Canonical Opportunity's own six-family union — not a sixth new category invented for this document, but the recognition that this union already exists as data and has simply never been given a governed publication boundary.

## 5. Why none of the existing owners should absorb this

**Evidence** cannot: its definition (`DOMAIN_MODEL.md`: *"A traceable representation of Reality from an attributable source"*) is about source fidelity, not reconciliation. A Requirement is already deduplicated and classified — an Evidence-*derived* fact, not Evidence itself. Absorbing it would mean Evidence performing reconciliation and conflict resolution, which its own architecture never claims and which would collapse the fidelity/reconciliation boundary every other domain in this repository is built to preserve.

**Canonical Opportunity** cannot: it already, explicitly, by name, excludes this exact content (`CANONICAL_OPPORTUNITY_PUBLICATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §5). Its own purpose statement is narrower and different in kind: *"Canonical does not mean infallible or complete. It means that downstream work has one governed authority for the present evidence state"* (`docs/architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md:59`) — for the six families it already owns, which describe *what and who* the opportunity is, not *what it demands*. Absorbing five new, cross-referential, differently-structured families would dilute an already-ratified, deliberately narrow authority boundary rather than extend it cleanly.

**Opportunity Intelligence** (the analyst, `opportunity_intelligence.py`) cannot: it is the domain's *interpretive* layer, and `docs/architecture/DECISION_DOCTRINE.md`'s central rule is exactly the one this would violate — *"Computation does not receive confidence... Confidence belongs to the reasoning, never to the source fact."* A Requirement's text and classification are facts, requiring zero interpretation; `opportunity_intelligence.py`'s computed facts, inferences, and hypotheses *about* requirements are a distinct, downstream layer that already, correctly, refuses to contain recommendations or decisions. Folding raw structural publication into the interpretive analyst would mix exactly the two authority levels every other part of this doctrine keeps separate, inside the one module built specifically to keep them separate.

## 6. The publication contract that would naturally emerge

Modeled directly on `CANONICAL_OPPORTUNITY_PUBLICATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`, since the same reasoning that produced that contract applies here without modification:

- **Responsibilities:** accept one completely validated, reconciled procurement-structure state under one exact supported contract version; preserve identity and authoritative input binding; publish every eligible object exactly once, preserving its already-assigned stable ID; expose every semantic field needed to interpret it without consumer inference; preserve classification, provenance, conflict state, and governed absence (including the same `PARTIAL`/`UNVERIFIED` accommodation Canonical Opportunity Publication now has, for the identical reason — these facts carry the identical tri-state provenance model, produced by the identical Stage A/B provenance logic); declare cross-reference and conflict-member relationships already present in the reconciled state; produce deterministic object digests, snapshot identity, and manifest ordering; validate complete membership and relationship closure; emit no publication when identity, semantics, or required closure cannot be proven.
- **Identifiers:** reuse Stage A's existing stable IDs (`req_id`, `criterion_id`, `clause_id`, `deliverable_id`, `submission_rule_id`) where present, falling back to the same deterministic hash-derived scheme `opportunity_intelligence.py`'s `_record_id` already implements — no new identifier scheme needs inventing.
- **Versioning:** its own `OWNER_DOMAIN` / `OWNER_CONTRACT` / `PUBLICATION_CONTRACT_VERSION` triple beginning at `1.0.0`, independent of Evidence's and Canonical Opportunity's own versions, governed by the same `GOVERNANCE.md` discipline used for every other contract in this repository.
- **Evidence ownership:** none directly — it declares relationships to Evidence Publication's governed references (via each object's `evidence_ids`/`source_refs`, exactly as Canonical Opportunity's observations already do) and, where a structural item depends on a canonical fact (e.g., a submission rule referencing the submission deadline), to Canonical Opportunity Publication's governed references. It never republishes evidence content itself.
- **Governed references:** participates in the existing, already domain-neutral `GOVERNED_REFERENCE_RESOLUTION_ARCHITECTURE.md` protocol without any special-casing — the protocol was built generically for exactly this kind of new owner to be added.
- **Downstream consumers:** as listed in §3 — Opportunity Intelligence, Opportunity Orchestration, Executive Opportunity Understanding, and (in the future) Proposal Compliance Intelligence.

## 7. Relationship to Clarification Questions and Amendments

Requirements are not static — the commissioning prompt is right to insist this be addressed rather than assumed away.

**Unknowns and Clarification Questions belong outside this owner, by the same reasoning that already excludes them from Canonical Opportunity.** `CANONICAL_OPPORTUNITY_PUBLICATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §5 excludes "unknowns... management questions" from Canonical Opportunity for a reason that applies identically here: an Unknown is an *interpretive act of noticing a gap*, and `docs/architecture/DOMAIN_MODEL.md` assigns Unknown's ownership to *"the layer that discovers it"* as a general rule, not as license for every discovering layer to also become Unknown's permanent home. Opportunity Structure's job is to publish the reconciled *fact* that a Requirement is `CONFLICTED` or carries an unresolved cross-document discrepancy (exactly parallel to how Canonical Opportunity already publishes `conflict_ids` and conflict state as fact, without owning the Management Question that might later be raised about that conflict). The Clarification Question *itself* — the governed, externally-directed knowledge-acquisition object — remains Opportunity Intelligence's responsibility, consistent with the constitutional review that already established this distinction (`OPPORTUNITY_INTELLIGENCE_ANALYST_SPECIFICATION.md` §18: *"A clarification question seeks an authoritative factual answer from the procurement authority... distinct from management questions"*).

**Amendments, supersession, split, and merge belong inside this owner**, for the same reason Canonical Opportunity already owns supersession for its own six families. `BID_LIFECYCLE_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s existing rule already names the mechanism precisely: *"An attributable procurement amendment enters Acquisition and Evidence first... impact is evaluated by the owning canonical and intelligence capabilities... It never silently changes a deadline, Requirement, Brief, proposal, approval, or decision. Unaffected validated records remain available."* For a Requirement specifically, this owner is "the owning canonical capability" the passage refers to — it is where an amendment's impact on a specific Requirement, Evaluation Criterion, Commercial Clause, or Deliverable would be evaluated and expressed, using the identical accretion-not-mutation pattern Canonical Opportunity already implements: a new, distinctly identified record; an explicit `SUPERSEDES` relationship (reusing `canonical_opportunity.py`'s existing `SUPERSESSION_BASES` vocabulary, which is not family-specific in its definition); the prior record retained, marked superseded, never deleted. Split and merge of a Requirement have no existing precedent anywhere in the repository (the prior constitutional review found the same absence for Unknown) — this document does not invent an answer where none exists; it identifies this owner as the correct *location* for that decision to be made when it is, not the decision itself.

## 8. Future evolution

This owner is the natural, and arguably the *only structurally consistent*, home for amendment streams and Requirement-level supersession — because it is the layer that already reconciles the pre-amendment state, and `docs/architecture/DECISION_DOCTRINE.md`'s continuity principle (*"Evidence may be incomplete, duplicated, ambiguous, superseded, or contradictory. Those conditions are retained"*) applies to procurement structure exactly as it already applies to Evidence and to Canonical Opportunity's own facts.

It is *not* the natural home for the clarification-response *workflow*, contradiction *resolution* as an interpretive act, or procurement evolution *narrative* (why something changed, what it means) — those remain Opportunity Intelligence's and, ultimately, Human Deliberation's territory, exactly as the doctrine already separates fact from interpretation from decision everywhere else. What this owner must do is keep the *factual* record of structural change intact and referenceable — the same narrow, disciplined role Canonical Opportunity already plays for identity and milestone facts — so that Opportunity Intelligence, Clarification Questions, and eventual Proposal Compliance checking always have a single, governed, historically-complete structural record to reason from, without ever being tempted to reconstruct it themselves.
